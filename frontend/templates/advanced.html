How report-to-sweep works

When one person reports a message, we fingerprint the campaign from the normalised subject, the sender and the set of link hosts. Every later recipient is matched to the same cluster, so the warning shows “3 reports · 1 clicked” before they act.

  • Cluster, don't duplicate. A re-sent variant with a new subject prefix or a fresh lookalike domain still lands in the same cluster when the link hosts match.
  • Social proof is the strongest signal. People trust “17 others received this” more than any risk score.
  • Report latency beats detection accuracy. The metric that matters is minutes from delivery to first report.

What this engine actually does

01
Lexical — no network

IDN & homoglyphs, typosquatting against 180+ brands, brand-in-subdomain, @ decoys, ports, high-abuse TLDs, shorteners, entropy, double extensions, open-redirect wrappers. Runs in ~5 ms.

02
Infrastructure

RDAP registration age and registrar, DNS (A/MX/NS/SPF/DMARC), TLS issuer and certificate age. A brand-claiming domain with no MX is not a brand.

03
Reputation

Google Safe Browsing and VirusTotal with your own key. Missing keys are labelled — never silently treated as clean.

04
Click preview

Server-side redirect chain, login-form discovery, and whether a password field POSTs to a different domain. SSRF-guarded, JavaScript never executed.

05
Email forensics

SPF/DKIM/DMARC with real signature verification, Received-chain hop analysis, Reply-To and display-name spoofing, BEC lexicon, India scam corpus, attachment flags.

06
Organisation memory

First-contact senders and domains, vendor bank-detail diffs against a trusted baseline, time-of-click URL snapshots, and campaign clustering.

Deliberate choices

  • Progressive verdicts. Lexical answer in milliseconds, corroboration streams in after. A slow checker gets bypassed.
  • “Unknown” is a real answer. If infrastructure can't corroborate, we say so. An unexpandable short link is never “safe”.
  • Every verdict has reasons. Named signals, weights, thresholds and a receipt ID you can paste into a ticket.
  • Never clickable. Everything is defanged — hxxps://evil[.]com.
  • Teach, don't scold. Each signal carries the transferable lesson.

Deliberate non-features

  • No opaque “risk score 87”. Scores are derived from auditable signals.
  • No JS execution, no form submission. The preview reads HTML and describes it.
  • No AI chat bubble. An evidence chain with citations beats prose.
  • No silent green ticks. Degraded mode is labelled every time.
  • No auto-trust of an email's own numbers. A bank detail from an unverified message never becomes the baseline.

Known limits, stated rather than hidden

  • .eml only — .msg needs one more parser.
  • No image or QR decoding — attachment hashes are reported instead.
  • Public-suffix list is small and India-heavy — swap in the full PSL for wider coverage.
  • Single HTML GET preview — JS-gated and login-gated pages won't reveal themselves.
  • SQLite is fine for a desk; move to Postgres for multi-tenant.
